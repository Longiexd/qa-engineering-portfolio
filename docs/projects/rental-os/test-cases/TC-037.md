## Preconditions

Authorized staging session; disposable test records; record the deployed build and test account role. The original live UI did not expose an independently verified deployed SHA.

## Procedure

1. Record current theme and language; switch to French and light mode.
2. Open Analytics and a rental payment/return panel; inspect labels, validation messages, logo and controls.
3. Switch to dark mode, compare the logo/readability, then restore original language and theme.

## Expected result

French controls and validation should be translated consistently; black logo appears in light mode, white logo in dark mode. Historical observation was partial: English Payment and option-loading warning text remained.

## Recorded observation

Switching works; black logo is shown in light mode, white in dark. English Payment label and option-loading warning remain on French screens

## Limits and cleanup
